Each level goes one step deeper. Stop reading wherever it stops being useful.

Level 1: the idea in one sentence

Hold a few liquid Indonesian large-cap stocks that are rising, give more money to the calmer ones, sit in cash when the market is in stress, and trade only when the change is big enough to justify the fees.

Level 2: the daily routine (automatic)

  • During the session: every 30 minutes the bot checks prices and news for what it holds, and messages you only when something needs attention.
  • After the close: it fills yesterday's orders in its model account, reruns the model, and sets the next session's orders.
  • Forecast risk: how much each stock is likely to swing tomorrow (a GARCH model).
  • Check the market's mood: the IHSG's trend and expected volatility.
  • Choose: stocks in an uptrend, ranked by gain per unit of risk.
  • Size: calmer stocks get bigger positions; the portfolio is capped at a target level of swings.
  • Protect: a stock that falls too far from its peak is sold.

Level 3: the exact rulebook

  • Universe: 30 of the most liquid IDX large caps (listed under About).
  • Entry filter: price above its 200-day average and a positive 6-month return, at least a year listed, and a 60-day median trading value of Rp10 billion or more.
  • Ranking: 6-month return divided by forecast annual volatility. New positions come from the top 8; a holding is kept while it stays inside the top 16 and in an uptrend (v2, October 2026).
  • Position size: proportional to 1/volatility, at most 20% per stock, scaled to about 15% expected annual volatility, never more than 100% invested.
  • Market brake: if the equal-weighted basket of the 30 stocks closes below its 200-day average, at most 50% invested (v2 uses the basket instead of the IHSG, whose composition changes in October 2026).
  • Rebalance: weekly, after the last session of each week. Stops are checked every day.
  • No-trade band: skip resizing unless a position is off its target by more than half of the target (and at least 2% of the account).
  • Stress gauge (v3): a composite of seven stress readings, each turned into a z-score over the past year: basket realised volatility, the IHSG GARCH forecast, emerging-market implied volatility (Cboe VXEEM), the big banks lagging the basket (a foreign-flow proxy), the share of stocks down more than 3% or locked at a limit, USD/IDR volatility, and a Williams VIX Fix on the basket. Below 0.5 is normal (up to 100% invested, 15% volatility target); 0.5 to 1.5 is elevated (at most 70%, 10% target, no new positions); above 1.5 is risk-off (at most 30%). Around MSCI announcement dates the cap is 50%. The trend brake still applies on top.
  • Censored days: while IDX's lower price limit is 10% or tighter (7% from 19 October to 31 December 2026), daily moves are cut short; the volatility used for sizing and stops is floored at the 2-day realised volatility.
  • Exit: sell if the price closes below its 200-day average, or falls more than 2.5 × a normal week's move from its peak since it was bought.
  • Orders: whole lots of 100 shares; limit prices on valid IDX ticks inside the day's price limits (ARA/ARB); buys up to +1% from the close, sells down to −2%; never chase a stock locked at its upper limit.
  • Costs assumed: 0.19% on buys, 0.29% on sells (includes the 0.1% sales tax), plus 0.1% slippage each way.
  • News: scored and logged for monitoring; it does not trigger trades.

Level 4: the math

  • Returns: rt = ln(Pt / Pt−1) on dividend-adjusted prices; moves beyond ±35% (impossible on IDX) are treated as data errors.
  • Volatility: GJR-GARCH(1,1), σ²t = ω + (α + γ·1[εt−1 < 0])·ε²t−1 + β·σ²t−1, with Hansen skewed-t shocks, fitted by maximum likelihood.
  • Annualising: σyear = σday × √240.
  • Portfolio risk: σp = √(wᵀΣw), Σ = D·R·D, R being last year's correlation of the model's standardised shocks.
  • Scaling: k = min(cap, 15% ÷ σp).
  • Stop: exit when P < peak × (1 − 2.5 · σday · √5).

Level 5: why you should (and shouldn't) trust it

  • No peeking: the backtest refits yearly on past data only, trades at the next open, and pays costs.
  • Known flatterers: today's large caps chosen with hindsight; occasional Yahoo data errors; every rule variant tested inflates the best result.
  • News scores are saved every day so their value can be tested later on data no model saw in advance.
  • Rules change: the lower price limit widens back to 35/25/20% on 1 Jan 2027; order limits follow the dated rule table.
  • The model account is paper money. This is a research tool, not financial advice.
